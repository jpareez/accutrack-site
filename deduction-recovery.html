Sound Familiar?

The deductions add up faster than anyone’s contesting them.

Contesting deductions is a full-time job. So it gets done last, and the uncontested ones become permanent margin loss.

  • Retailers short-pay invoices for shortages, damages, and discounts you never agreed to.
  • Cash discounts get taken outside terms, and DSO quietly climbs.
  • Trade-fund and promo dollars go out, but nobody reconciles what actually came back.

Why The Margin Slips

Trade spend is your second-biggest line. It’s also the least watched.

Trade and promotional spend is often the largest CPG cost after the product itself. Retailers reclaim it through deductions, and the invalid, duplicate, and unauthorized ones slip through because no one has the capacity to fight each line.

What We Take Off Your Plate

One team. The whole recovery operation.

  • Research and resolve deductions for shortages, damages, pricing, and returns
  • Recover unearned cash discounts taken outside terms (and bring DSO down)
  • Audit and recover invalid, duplicate, and unauthorized trade deductions
  • Reconcile trade-fund and rebate programs against what was actually owed
  • File and chase freight and shipping claims on your behalf
  • Flag chronic short-payers before they cost you another quarter

Common Questions

Straight answers, no pitch.

What counts as a recoverable deduction?

Shortages, damages, pricing and unit discrepancies, returns, unearned cash discounts, and invalid or duplicate trade deductions. If it was taken outside the terms of sale, it’s in scope.

Is this contingency or a fee?

We price it after the assessment, based on volume and scope. You see the number before you commit to anything.

Do you replace our AR or finance team?

No. You keep every decision. We handle the deduction grind your team doesn’t have capacity to chase.

How is this different from deduction software?

Software flags a deduction. It doesn’t contest it, document it, or recover the money. We do the work and bring the dollars back.

Accu-Track Financial Solutions recovers the money retailers deduct off your invoices: invalid and unauthorized deductions, unearned cash discounts taken outside terms, and mismanaged trade and promotional funds. For consumer-product manufacturers this is often the largest uncontrolled leak in the operating margin.

Why deductions become permanent margin loss

Retailers reclaim trade spend through deductions for shortages, damages, pricing, returns, and promotions. Many are invalid, duplicate, or unauthorized, but contesting each line is a full-time job, so the uncontested ones quietly become permanent margin loss.

What we recover and run

We research and resolve deductions, recover unearned cash discounts and improve days sales outstanding, audit and recover invalid trade deductions, reconcile trade-fund and rebate programs, and file freight claims on your behalf. This complements our royalty reporting and recovery and full contract operations.

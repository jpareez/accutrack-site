Licensing agreement compliance services

Accu-Track Financial Solutions tracks every obligation, deadline, and requirement across your licensing portfolio so nothing slips through the cracks. We manage licensing compliance for both licensors and licensees, with proactive follow-up before deadlines become problems.

Why compliance fails

Obligations are scattered across agreements, spreadsheets, and inboxes, and deadlines surprise teams that have no system tracking them. The cost of a missed obligation keeps rising.

What we do

We catalog every obligation and deadline, monitor compliance on both sides of each agreement, follow up proactively, and report status clearly. This works hand in hand with royalty reporting and full contract management.

Audits and enforcement

We help prepare for and support licensing audits and track the results so issues are resolved, not forgotten.

Portfolio visibility

You get one clear view of where every agreement stands. See the impact in our case study.

Case Study

Bringing order to an inherited licensing portfolio.

A client inherited a portfolio nobody had organized. In four months, our team collected $27.6M and brought the whole thing to order. Here is how.

Request a Free Assessment →

No obligation. A 30-minute call with a contract operations specialist.

The Situation

No central record. No one connected.

A client inherited a contract and licensing portfolio with no central record. Nobody knew how many agreements existed, who was compliant, or whether royalty payments were accurate. Legal, finance, and the business units weren’t connected, and finance couldn’t reconcile payments against contracts they couldn’t see.

What We Did

One master database. Continuous contact.

We gathered every contract from legal, royalty accounting, and the business into one master database. We separated active agreements from expired ones, re-engaged active licensees with welcome packets, guaranteed royalty-reporting accuracy, and kept continuous contact to hold everyone to their obligations.

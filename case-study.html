Case Study

$27.6M recovered from a licensing portfolio nobody was watching.

A client inherited a contract and licensing portfolio with no central record. No one knew how many agreements existed or whether the royalties were even accurate. In four months, our team brought the whole thing to order and surfaced $27.6M that was sitting inside it. Here is how, along with a second engagement that proves the other half of the job.

The Situation

No central record. Nobody connected. Money leaking the whole time.

A client inherited a contract and licensing portfolio with no central record. Nobody knew how many agreements existed, who was compliant, or whether the royalty payments were accurate. Legal, finance, and the business units weren’t talking to each other, so finance was trying to reconcile payments against contracts they couldn’t even see. Money was slipping out the whole time, and there was no report that showed it.

What We Did

One master database. Active separated from expired. Continuous contact.

We pulled every contract from legal, royalty accounting, and the business into one master database. We separated active agreements from expired ones. We re-engaged the active licensees with welcome packets, locked down royalty-reporting accuracy, and kept continuous contact so everyone was held to their obligations instead of left to self-report and round down. For the first time, someone was actually running the desk.

Case Study Two · Capacity

They had the software. They didn’t have the staff.

The Situation

The systems were there. The capacity wasn’t.

This client already had a centralized contract list and a software platform, and they knew which licensees were not paying. What they did not have was the staff to actually run it. One licensee was out of compliance on its obligations, and nobody had the capacity to enforce it.

What We Did

Staff and structure, alongside their team.

We supplied the staff and structure to run the software and the process the way it was meant to run, working alongside the client’s existing team rather than over it. We became the client’s link and voice to the licensees on both financial and non-financial compliance, and worked across their business segments to bring everyone back into compliance on their obligations.

The Result

Their team went back to the core business.

The client now has a properly staffed process and a department that can resolve non-compliance and keep monitoring it. Their licensees got a single point of contact. And the client went from a struggling licensor to a valued licensee in its own right.

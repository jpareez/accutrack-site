The Story

From recovering held-back receivables to running entire contract operations.

Accu-Track started in 1990 as Credit & Financial Services, helping consumer-product manufacturers recover money retailers were quietly holding back: short-paid invoices, unearned discounts, undocumented deductions. In the late ’80s and early ’90s, that practice tied up 10% or more of a manufacturer’s receivables. Finding money that was already owed but quietly slipping away was the whole job. It still is.

Our first major client was Sundor Brands, later acquired by Procter & Gamble. That work built our reputation for detail and dispute resolution. Over the next three decades we expanded into contract management, licensing compliance, and business process outsourcing for Fortune 500 brands.

In 2018 we became Accu-Track Financial Solutions. The work is the same as it’s always been: we handle the operational details other teams don’t have time for, we find the money sitting inside contracts nobody is tracking, and we get the numbers right.

Our history

We began in 1990 helping consumer-product manufacturers recover money retailers held back through short-paid invoices and undocumented deductions. Our first major client, Sundor Brands, was later acquired by Procter & Gamble. We rebranded as Accu-Track Financial Solutions in 2018.

What we do today

We provide royalty reporting, licensing compliance, contract management, and post-merger cleanup for US enterprise and mid-market clients.

Why clients stay

Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

How is this different from CLM software?

Software gives you a platform. We give you a team. Tools work well if you have the people to run them, but if your bottleneck is capacity, software just adds work. Accu-Track handles the operations directly, with no implementation, no training, and no new system to learn.

What types of contracts do you manage?

Licensing agreements, royalty-bearing contracts, and complex multi-party arrangements, including licensing compliance, royalty reporting and reconciliation, obligation tracking, renewal management, and post-acquisition consolidation.

How big does my portfolio need to be?

We work best with organizations managing 50 or more active contracts or licensing agreements. If it fits in a spreadsheet, we are probably not the right fit.

Do you replace our finance or legal team?

No. We take the operational load off them so they focus on higher-value work. You keep all decision-making authority; we handle the execution.

What does it cost?

It depends on volume, complexity, and scope. We build a custom proposal after the assessment. Most clients find outsourcing costs less than hiring even one full-time contract administrator.

How quickly can you start?

Most engagements are operational within 2 to 4 weeks. For urgent situations like post-acquisition cleanup, we can accelerate.

What happens on the free assessment?

A 30-minute call where a specialist reviews your contract landscape, finds where the pain is, and gives an honest read on whether outsourcing makes sense. No pitch.

Outsourced contract management, royalty reporting, and licensing compliance

Accu-Track Financial Solutions runs the operational side of contracts and licensing agreements for companies that manage complex portfolios. For more than three decades we have handled royalty reporting and reconciliation, licensing compliance, contract administration, and post-acquisition contract cleanup, so finance and legal teams stop drowning in operational detail and focus on growth.

What outsourced contract operations means

Outsourcing contract operations means a dedicated, experienced team performs the day-to-day work of managing your contracts and licensing agreements: calculating and verifying royalties, tracking obligations and deadlines, administering renewals and amendments, and producing clean, reliable reporting. You keep every decision; we handle the execution. Learn more about our contract and licensing operations services.

A specialist team, not software

Contract-lifecycle-management software gives you a platform you still have to staff and run. If your real constraint is capacity, software adds work. Accu-Track is the outsourced contract management alternative that simply does the work, with no implementation and no new system to learn.

Royalty reporting you can trust

Inaccurate or late royalty numbers create real financial exposure. Our royalty reporting and reconciliation work verifies that every payment matches agreement terms and recovers revenue that has been paid late, short, or not at all.

Licensing compliance across the portfolio

Every licensing agreement carries obligations and deadlines. Our licensing compliance service tracks all of them and follows up proactively so nothing lapses.

Order after a merger or acquisition

Acquisitions bring inherited contracts with no central record. Our post-merger contract management brings those portfolios into one organized system, fast. See the results in our licensing portfolio case study.

Privacy Policy

Effective April 6, 2026. Credit & Financial Services Inc. dba Accu-Track Financial Solutions.

This Privacy Policy explains how Credit & Financial Services Inc., doing business as Accu-Track Financial Solutions ("Accu-Track," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards personal information when you visit this website or contact us through it. By using this site or submitting a form, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Credit & Financial Services Inc., doing business as Accu-Track Financial Solutions, is located in Glastonbury, Connecticut. You can reach us at info@accutracksolutions.com or (860) 236-8002.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide. When you complete a form or contact us, we collect details such as your name, work email address, company name, phone number, and information about your contract or licensing operations (for example, the number of agreements you manage).
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, we and our service providers may automatically collect technical and usage data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring URLs, and similar analytics data, including marketing identifiers such as click identifiers (for example, a GCLID) and campaign parameters (UTM tags) used to measure advertising performance.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a consultation or assessment.
  • To provide, operate, and improve our services and this website.
  • To send you communications you have requested or that relate to your inquiry, including by email and SMS (see below).
  • To measure and improve the performance of our marketing and advertising.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

SMS and text messaging

If you provide a mobile number and check the consent box on our form, you agree to receive SMS and email communications from Accu-Track regarding your inquiry, including follow-ups, appointment scheduling, reminders, meeting links, and service updates. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, and third-party analytics and advertising services (including Google Analytics and Google Ads), to understand how the site is used and to measure advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business and communicate with you (for example, hosting, customer-relationship, analytics, and advertising providers), who are obligated to protect it and use it only to provide services to us. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data retention

We keep personal information as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Data security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, or to update your information, contact us at info@accutracksolutions.com. You may opt out of SMS by replying STOP and unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any such email.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective" date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.

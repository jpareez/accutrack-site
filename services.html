Contract and licensing operations services

Accu-Track Financial Solutions provides outsourced contract and licensing operations: royalty reporting and reconciliation, licensing compliance, contract administration and obligation management, post-acquisition contract cleanup, and centralized portfolio reporting. One team runs the operational work so your staff focuses on higher-value priorities.

About these services

Royalty reporting and reconciliation

We prepare accurate, on-time, audit-ready royalty reports, reconcile payments against agreement terms, and flag discrepancies. Details on the royalty reporting service page.

Outsourced contract management

Renewals, amendments, terminations, obligation tracking, and reporting, handled by a team that knows your agreements. This is the work CLM software leaves you to staff.

Licensing compliance

We track every obligation and deadline across your licensing portfolio with proactive follow-up. See the licensing compliance page.

Post-acquisition contract cleanup

We consolidate and organize inherited contract portfolios after a merger or acquisition. See post-merger contract management.

Centralized visibility and reporting

One clear view of your entire contract landscape: what is active, what is due, and what needs attention. Proof of impact is in our case study.

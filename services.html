What We Do

The whole operation, handled.

Royalty Reporting, Reconciliation & Recovery

Licensees self-report what they owe you, and the number usually comes in rounded down. We reconcile every payment against the actual contract and recover what was short, late, or missed.

Explore →

Deduction & Trade-Fund Recovery

Retailers claw money back off your invoices through deductions, unearned discounts, and trade-fund claims, and most of it goes uncontested. We investigate every deduction, recover what was taken invalidly, and run the trade-fund program so the money stops walking.

Explore →

Outsourced Contract Management

This is most of what we do: renewals, amendments, obligations, and the daily grind of running your agreements. It’s the work CLM software hands back to you, except we do it, with no system for your people to babysit.

Explore →

Licensing Compliance & Administration

Every obligation and deadline tracked across your portfolio, with follow-up before anything lapses. Audit clauses enforced and reporting requirements held, so the terms you negotiated actually get honored.

Contract and licensing operations services

Royalty reporting and reconciliation

We prepare accurate, on-time, audit-ready royalty reports, reconcile payments against agreement terms, and flag discrepancies. Details on the royalty reporting service page.

Outsourced contract management

Renewals, amendments, terminations, obligation tracking, and reporting, handled by a team that knows your agreements. This is the work CLM software leaves you to staff.

Licensing compliance

We track every obligation and deadline across your licensing portfolio with proactive follow-up. See the licensing compliance page.

Post-acquisition contract cleanup

We consolidate and organize inherited contract portfolios after a merger or acquisition. See post-merger contract management.

Centralized visibility and reporting
